Part 3 — Tips inside lessons (written in simple English)

#LessonTipCheckCorrection / comment
1Consonant SoundsPunjabi has aspirated and unaspirated sounds, so the English puff of air will feel familiar. Watch these: English t and d are made on the ridge behind your teeth — not curled back (ਟ, ਡ) and not on the teeth (ਤ, ਦ). /f/ is not ਫ (an aspirated p). /z/ is not ਜ (“jero” → zero ). And no extra vowel before s + consonant: “ischool”, “sakool” → school .☐ OK
☐ Fix
2TH: think and thisMany Punjabi speakers use dental ਥ and ਦ for TH (“tink”, “dis”). That’s close! Push your tongue a little further forward — between your teeth — and let the air flow out.☐ OK
☐ Fix
3W vs VPunjabi ਵ is between English W and V, so wine/vine and west/vest can sound the same. For W, round your lips — no teeth. For V, put your top teeth on your bottom lip.☐ OK
☐ Fix
4Final Consonants: bag / backWatch final consonant groups and -ed: worked = “workt” (one syllable), asked = “askt”.☐ OK
☐ Fix
5Short and Long Vowels: ship / sheepKeep /iː/ long and smiling ( sheep, leave ), and /ɪ/ short and relaxed ( ship, live ). Punjabi ਈ and ਇ are a good starting point.☐ OK
☐ Fix
6cat / cut / betMany Punjabi speakers say /æ/ like ਐ or /e/, so bad sounds like bed and man like men . Open your jaw wide for cat .☐ OK

7Rhythm: English Keeps the BeatPunjabi rhythm gives syllables more equal time, and Punjabi uses tone. In English, one syllable is long, loud and high, and the small words become very short.☐ OK
☐ Fix
8Articles: a, an, the — or nothingPunjabi has no articles, so a/an/the are often dropped: “I am going to office” → I’m going to the office ; “He is doctor” → He’s a doctor .☐ OK

9Present: Simple vs ContinuousAnother common pattern: state verbs with -ing — “I am knowing”, “I am having a car”, “I am understanding”. In Canadian English: I know. I have a car. I understand.☐ OK

10Present Perfect vs Past SimpleA common pattern from Indian English: “I am living here since two years.” In Canadian English: I’ve lived here for two years or I’ve been living here for two years .☐ OK
